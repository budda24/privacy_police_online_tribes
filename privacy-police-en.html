Privacy Policy for Online Tribes

Effective Date: 28.08.2026

At Damian Jablonski Tribe, we take the privacy of our users seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use Online Tribes (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the App, we may collect the following types of information:

1.1 Personal Information

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect information such as your name, username, profile picture, bio, email address, gender, and any other information you provide during registration.
  • Profile Information: You can upload a profile picture, edit your bio, and update other personal details within the app.
  • Tribe Information: When you create or join a tribe, we collect details such as tribe name, tribe type, themes, criteria, and membership rules.
  • Messages and Communication: We collect content from your direct messages, group chats, and tribe interactions, including any multimedia shared (photos, videos, files).

1.2 Usage Data

  • Posts and Interactions: We collect data on the posts you create, like, comment on, and share, as well as tribes you create or participate in.
  • Activity Logs: We track your usage activity, including tribe searches, tribe invitations, tribe requests, and activity charts showing user and tribe activity.
  • Device Information: We collect information about the device you use to access the App, including your IP address, device type, operating system, and browser type.

1.3 Payment Information

  • Subscription Plans: When you subscribe to a paid plan, we collect payment information (such as credit card details or other payment methods) through our secure payment gateway.
  • Transaction History: We retain a history of all payments and transactions made within the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To Provide and Maintain the App: We use your information to create and manage your account, facilitate tribe creation and participation, and enable core app functionality such as posting, messaging, tribe management, and notifications.
  • To Improve User Experience: We analyze usage data to understand how users interact with the App and improve features such as tribe recommendations, search filters, and tribe invitations.
  • To Process Payments: We use your payment information to process subscription payments and provide access to premium features.
  • To Communicate with You: We send push notifications and emails related to app activity, such as tribe invitations, direct messages, and tribe updates.
  • To Ensure Safety and Security: We monitor activity to detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or any activities that violate our terms of service.

3. Identity Verification and Face Data

To keep Online Tribes free of bots, fake profiles and impersonation, we ask every member to complete a one-time liveness check before they can take part in the community. You are told what the check involves and must actively choose to start it; nothing is captured until you do.

3.1 What is collected

  • Liveness check: a short selfie capture, used only to confirm that a real, live person is present.
  • Optional identity verification: if you choose to verify your identity, images of a government-issued ID document are captured and compared against your selfie.

This is biometric data and a special category of personal data under Article 9 GDPR. We process it on the basis of your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you may withdraw at any time.

3.2 Who processes it

The capture takes place inside the software development kit of our identity verification provider, Didit (didit.me), and is sent directly to Didit, which acts as our data processor under a Data Processing Agreement concluded in accordance with Article 28 GDPR. Didit stores this data within the European Union, encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3), and is certified to SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

3.3 What we store

Online Tribes never receives, processes or stores your facial image, video, or any biometric template. We receive only the outcome of the check, and store: whether the liveness check was passed, whether identity verification was passed, the date of verification, and the provider's session identifier. If you complete identity verification we also store your date of birth, age and gender as read from the document. Face data is never displayed on your profile or shown to other members.

3.4 Retention and deletion

Raw selfie and liveness media are held by our provider for no longer than 12 months and are then automatically deleted. The verification result we hold is kept for as long as your account exists, and is deleted when you delete your account. You may request erasure at any time at franek@online-tribes.com.

3.5 What it is not used for

Face data is never used for facial recognition against other members' photos, for tagging, for advertising, profiling or targeting, and it is never sold or shared with any party other than the processor named above.

4. Advertising and Tracking

The App displays advertising provided by Google AdMob. Depending on the choices you make, advertising may be personalised or non-personalised.

  • Your choice: the first time you open the App you are asked, through Google's consent flow, whether you agree to personalised advertising. On iOS you are additionally asked for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change either choice at any time in Settings.
  • Non-personalised advertising: if you decline, you will still see advertising, but it will not be based on your interests or advertising identifiers.
  • Analytics and attribution: we use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and AppsFlyer to understand how the App is used and how installations are attributed, and Meta App Events for advertising measurement.

We never sell your personal data, and we never use the face data described in Section 3 for advertising in any form.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except in the following cases:

  • With Service Providers: We may share your information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating the App, processing payments, and improving functionality (e.g., Firebase, payment processors).
  • For Legal Purposes: We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to valid legal requests (e.g., subpoenas, court orders).
  • With Your Consent: We may share your information with other users or third parties when you provide explicit consent (e.g., when sharing posts or tribes publicly).

6. Data Retention

We will retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the App’s services. We may also retain certain information for legal, tax, or audit purposes.

7. Your Rights

As a user of the App, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access and Rectification: You can access and update your personal information, including profile details, at any time through your account settings.
  • Data Deletion: You may request that we delete your account and all associated personal data by contacting us at franek@online-tribes.com.
  • Withdraw Consent: You can withdraw consent for the collection and use of your data at any time by closing your account.

8. Security

We use industry-standard encryption technologies and security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children’s Privacy

Our App is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete that information.
